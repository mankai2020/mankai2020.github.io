2026 Climate Dynamics

Tropical oceans drive decadal trends in surface mass balance over Wilkes Land, East Antarctica

Kai Man, Xichen Li, Yetang Wang, Guitao Shi, Lei Geng, Yonghao Wang, Yujie Miao & Weihan Ma

Ice-core records, reconstructions, and atmospheric model experiments show that tropical Atlantic and Indian Ocean warming contributed to declining surface mass balance over Wilkes Land by altering atmospheric circulation and moisture transport.

2026 Environmental Research Letters

Uncertainty in Antarctic precipitation projections under global warming

Kai Man, Yujie Miao, Yonghao Wang, Yusi Feng & Xichen Li

A CMIP6 multi-model analysis reveals substantial uncertainty in future Antarctic precipitation, linked to differences in surface warming, atmospheric circulation, and tropical sea-surface temperature projections.

Century-long West Antarctic snow accumulation changes induced by tropical teleconnections

Kai Man, Jürg Luterbacher, David M. Holland, Naiming Yuan, Lei Geng, Yetang Wang, Yonggang Liu, Guitao Shi, Yurong Hou, Wenju Cai & Xichen Li

Ice-core evidence and numerical experiments connect century-long changes in tropical Atlantic and Pacific temperatures to contrasting snow accumulation trends across West Antarctica.
